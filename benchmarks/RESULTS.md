Notes

- ± is the standard deviation across folds.
- *p vs #1*: corrected resampled t-test on per-fold macro-F1 against the top row. p ≥ 0.05 means the gap is not distinguishable from fold noise with this much data.
- Log loss measures probability quality (lower is better); n/a for models without probabilities.
- Fit and predict times were measured on: Hugging Face Jobs a10g-small (1x NVIDIA A10G, 4 vCPU). Predict is per document on unseen strings, batch of 200; transformer inference on CPU is far slower.
- Size is the trained artifact; for pretrained encoders/vectors it is their parameter memory.
